How to convert

inches = centimeters ÷ 2.54

A height of 180 cm: 180 ÷ 2.54 = 70.87 in, which is 5 ft 10.87 in, or 5 ft 10 ⅞ in to the nearest sixteenth. Going the other way, multiply by 2.54: 6 in × 2.54 = 15.24 cm.

One inch has been 2.54 cm since 1959

Before 1959 the US and the UK each kept an inch of their own, a few millionths apart. The international yard and pound agreement fixed the inch at exactly 2.54 cm, so every conversion on this page is exact until it is rounded. The one survivor, the US survey foot, differed by two parts in a million and was retired at the end of 2022; it matters for land surveys, never for a height, a screen, or a parcel.

Where it comes up: heights (the US and the UK say 5 ft 11 in, most of the world 180 cm), screens and paper sizes, shoe lasts, and anything measured with a tape that shows sixteenths.
