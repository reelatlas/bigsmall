How to Find Your Size
Step 1. Find a hard flat surface, tape a piece of blank paper, flush against a wall. Place your foot on the paper, with your heel flush against the wall, stand up straight.
Step 2. Have a friend mark the longest part of your foot (referred to as heel-to-toe length) on the paper with a pen or pencil, or measure yourself if necessary. Repeat with the other foot, as right and left sizes may be different.
Step 3. Use a ruler to measure the heel-to-toe length you marked for each foot.
Wide Width Sizes
Wide width sizes include more volume and broader platform by approximately 15mm to the ball and instep and 6mm to the bottom width.
