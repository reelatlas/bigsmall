## How to Measure (the panel's three steps, from screenshot-03)

Step 1: Place sheet of paper next to the wall. Stand on the paper with your heels against the wall.
Step 2: Mark the end of the longest toe of each foot.
Step 3: Mark the widest point at the left and right side of the ball of your feet.
