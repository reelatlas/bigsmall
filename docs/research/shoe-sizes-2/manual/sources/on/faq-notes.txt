How do I know which size I need?
If you're unsure which size you wear in our footwear or apparel, each product page has a size chart and fit recommendation. The Japanese (JP) sizing is in centimeters, so you can also measure your foot by standing barefoot with your heel against a wall, adding about 1 cm to your measured size for an indication of what size will work for you.

What is the difference in sizing between men's and women's footwear?
The primary difference is that men's shoes are designed wider than women's. Customers are free to purchase shoes of any gender, though! Women's shoes are a 1.5 size larger than men's (so a men's size 7 is a women's 8.5 in US sizes). We just recommend testing it out first at a retail location if possible.

Do you offer wide sizing?
We have certain models that are available in wide sizes. To find these select styles and colors for men and women, simply filter using the "Wide Fit" filter on the left-hand side of any product listing page. Not available in all countries.

I live in Australia or New Zealand - are your shoe sizes on the website shown in US or UK sizes?
Our Australian and New Zealand sites show shoes in US sizes.

What it says for the record: On's JP label is the measured foot plus about a centimetre, by the brand's own rule, so a typed foot length can reach an On size through its words (docs/research/shoe-sizes-2.md §9); the FAQ's "women's are 1.5 sizes larger than men's" contradicts the product panels' charts, where US women's = US men's + 1 at the same JP label (manual/brand-charts.json, the on quirks); both are kept as published.
