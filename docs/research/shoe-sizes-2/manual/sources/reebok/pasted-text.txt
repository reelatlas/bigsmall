How to Measure
Step 1. Place a piece of paper on a hard floor with one end to the wall. Stand on the piece of paper with the heel slightly touching the wall.
Step 2. Mark the end of your longest toe with a pencil for both feet. Tip: it's easier if you let someone help you with this.
Step 3. Measure your markings in inches. Take the larger of the two foot length values and compare it with our sizing chart.
