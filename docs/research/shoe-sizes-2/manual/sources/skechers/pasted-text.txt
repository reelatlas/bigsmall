## Find Your Perfect Fit (the width panel, from the screenshot)

Every foot is unique so we've developed a signature fit system to help you find your favorite style in the best size to ensure the greatest comfort possible.
Skechers Classic Fit: Men's D Width, Women's B Width. Medium width shoes with a snug and even fit from heel to toe.
Relaxed Fit from Skechers: Men's D/E Width, Women's B/C Width. Maintains medium width in the heel with a roomier toe box.
Skechers Wide Fit: Men's 2E/3E Width, Women's D Width. A bit more open interior offers a looser fit around the entire foot.
Skechers Extra Wide Fit: Men's 4E/5E Width. Maximum space means wider feet stay comfortable all day.
