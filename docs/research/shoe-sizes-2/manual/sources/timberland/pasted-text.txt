How to Measure for the Right Fit
1 Place a piece of paper that's larger than your foot against the wall.
2 Stand with one foot on the paper, with your heel just up against the wall.
3 Mark the end of your longest toe on the paper (you may need a friend to help) and measure from the wall to the marking.
4 Do the same for the other foot and compare measurements with our size chart to get the right size!
