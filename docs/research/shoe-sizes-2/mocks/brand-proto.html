One foot, every brand

Type a size you fit with its brand (nike 10), a size alone (us 9, eu 43), or your foot's length heel to toe (27 cm). The scale rows show one chart, ISO's or the brand's own; each brand's row is that brand's chart read at the same foot. Tap any row to put it in the box, as in the product.

What to try

  • You wear a US 9. us 9: ISO's scales, and under them what each brand's chart makes of that foot. Then nike 9 or new balance 9 to start from a shoe you actually own, and watch the other brands move.
  • You measured your foot. 27 cm: your size at every brand that gives a foot length; the four that give none say so.
  • Women's. nike women 8 or women 8: the women's charts answer, a different last at Nike.
  • Edges. converse 10 (the brand that runs large), puma 10 then 27 cm puma (a chart with no foot length), reebok 10 (no UK or EU printed), birkenstock eu 42 (ranges as printed), nike 10 to eu (a target).

Not in this prototype

  • The children's scales and their beyond rows; hiding rows and Edit; Display's fractions and sizing up; the shortcuts map and copying on a second tap; the URL.
  • The product's own section order (the visitor's system first), aliases beyond those listed, and typo tolerance.
  • A brand's exact printed form for a range is drawn here from its two ends (8–8.5); the product would print the brand's text.
  • A brand's second line (Converse's Standard table), widths, and the second batch of brands.
