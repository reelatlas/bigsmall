HOW TO MEASURE YOUR FEET
Remember to measure both feet, making a note of the longest.

Place your foot on a flat surface with your heel against a straight edge.

Place a ruler beside your foot touching the straight edge your heel is also touching, then take the length in millimetres from the tip of your longest toe to your heel.

(Please be aware that your longest toe may not always be your big toe.)

*These measurements do not correlate to measuring with a Clarks foot gauge. For this method, follow these instructions.
