Find Your Perfect Fit

Every foot is unique so we've developed a signature fit system to help you find your favorite style in the best size to ensure the greatest comfort possible.

Men's D Width / Women's B Width: Medium width shoes with a snug and even fit from heel to toe.
Men's D/E Width / Women's B/C Width: Maintains medium width in the heel with a roomier toe box.
Men's 2E/3E Width / Women's D Width: A bit more open interior offers a looser fit around the entire foot.
Men's 4E/5E Width: Maximum space means wider feet stay comfortable all day.
