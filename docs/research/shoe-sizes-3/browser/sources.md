## What an account, a permission, or a click by the owner would unlock

- **Extension site permissions** (the owner can grant them in the Claude in Chrome extension): skechers.com.au (the AU size guide with its CM figures), support.newbalance.com (the fit article), reebok.co.uk (read permission; its size guide), clarks.co.uk (the kids' calculator).
- **Birkenstock's calculator** needs each foot's length and width chosen by hand in four dropdowns and "CALCULATE SIZE"; no account. The owner can probe it in a minute, or a later pass can click each select (about 5 calls per foot length).
- **True Fit** on HOKA and UGG ("Get Started / Sign In"): a profile built from shoes owned; it asks for a sign-in or personal details, so not used.
- **New Balance's printable measuring tools** (PDF downloads on the size guide): need the owner's yes to download.
- No login or membership wall stood in front of any chart read here.

## Notes on the record

- charts.csv: for a chart printed in rows (On, HOKA, Crocs, Asics MY) "column" holds the row's label and "row" the cell's position from 1; for a chart printed in columns (UGG, HOKA Kids) "column" is the column header and "row" the row index.
- Not transcribed to charts.csv (held already, and their length column is in the page files): adidas's Heel-toe chart; New Balance's Length tables (not said to be the foot's); Asics US CM; Skechers US; Reebok EU (Europe/UK/US only, in the page file).
- On's charts print a BR row and an MX row (MX equal to JP cell by cell) which the On chart held may lack; recorded for that reason, though neither is a foot length.
- HOKA's All Gender chart prints 21 foot-length and width cells against 22 sizes; the cells are recorded in printed order, unaligned, as printed.
- The screenshot of On's women's chart did not save (the tool returned an image error).
