# Worker C, the leads: findings (2026-10-21)

The report as the worker returned it, saved by the orchestrator (the `orchestrate` skill, §4). 110 tool calls as the harness counted them (the worker counted about 117), 238,898 tokens, about 24 minutes.

I reached 26 kinds of third-party source across all twenty brands. Only a few measured anything themselves, and none of those measured a foot.

**Which sources measured, which compiled, which only opine**

- **Measured, but small or self-reported:**
  - Zappos's customer fit answers. The product pages carry a `sizeRatingCounts` field with buyers' own answers ("Felt true to size", "Felt a half size larger than marked", and so on). It is the only body of numbers from many wearers, but it is per product, not per brand.
  - Stridewise's author gives his own Brannock reading for one pair of Dr. Martens and one of Timberlands. That is one foot.
  - RunRepeat says it measures internal shoe length in its lab, but only in its model reviews. Its size-chart pages are compiled.
- **Compiled from the brands' own charts, and saying so:**
  - RunRepeat: "We used the data from 40+ brand shoe size charts".
  - size.ly: "Matched by foot length from both brands' published size charts · Updated July 2026".
  - AlpinStore: "Sources: official UGG size charts (help.ugg.com, ugg.com)".
  - Footwear Magazine, which says its rows compare size labels, "not measured-foot-length equivalents".
- **Compiled without saying from where:** the retailer charts at Scheels, SportsShoes.com, Outsole.nl, Foot Locker UK and Zappos (generic chart), the size-chart site LookSize, and Flight Club (a generic chart).
- **Opinion only:** Hibbett, Man of Many, Captain Creps, OPUMO, Bootspy, SoleFeed, Fit2Run, Road Runner Sports, Tom's Guide, Slickdeals, and Outsole's fit text, which judges every brand against its own Air Max 1 range.

**The forums could not be reached.** Reddit refused all three ways I tried. QVC's community and The Iconic answered with Cloudflare checks. So there are no agree/disagree counts from Reddit at all.

## Claims that recur, per brand

- **Nike.** The sources split:
  - RunRepeat: "nearly all Nike shoes we've tested proved to be true to size", after it "used to be notorious for running small".
  - Outsole: "slightly narrower and smaller".
  - Hibbett: "runs a little larger than the Three Stripes… take a half-size smaller in Nikes".
  - Man of Many calls Nike "the gold standard" and also says "Nike shoes are known to run small".
  - size.ly's foot-length tables put Nike about a full size, or 1.5, above Skechers, New Balance, Puma and Under Armour at the same foot.
  - RunRepeat's tables put women's Nike half a size above adidas, New Balance and HOKA.
  - Footwear Magazine: Nike "distinguishes the CM/JP number printed on a box from its measured foot-length column."
- **adidas.** True to size in RunRepeat and Outsole. "Samba and Campus 00s can feel slightly small" (Outsole). Ultraboost "a size up" (Man of Many, quoting a retailer's fitter).
- **New Balance.** True to size in RunRepeat and Outsole. "A few millimetres bigger than adidas" (Man of Many). size.ly: "New Balance fits about a full size bigger" than Nike.
- **Puma.** Women's are half a size off adidas and Reebok "because PUMA has a 1.5 US size difference" (RunRepeat). "Suede run slightly small" (Outsole). size.ly: Puma 10 is about Nike 11.
- **Asics.** RunRepeat's table puts Asics men's at 11.5 where every other brand is 11 at 290 mm. "Size up half a size when in doubt" (Outsole). "Tends to run snug" (Fit2Run).
- **Converse.** The most consistent finding across sources: Chucks run half a size large. RunRepeat's table gives Chuck Taylor 8.5 against a regular 9. Also "runs half a size larger" (Outsole), "opting for a half size down" (OPUMO), and Man of Many and Captain Creps say the same.
- **Vans.** True to size (Outsole, Captain Creps). RunRepeat: one unisex last means "a slight (half-a-size) deviation".
- **Skechers.** RunRepeat: "0.5 to 1 US size discrepancies with Nike and adidas". size.ly: a full size bigger than Nike. Outsole: true to size.
- **Reebok.** RunRepeat says it shares adidas's scheme, but its Reebok page also states a 1.5-size women's gap that its own table contradicts. Outsole: true to size on lifestyle models.
- **Under Armour.** "Extensively praised for running true to size" (RunRepeat). "True to size or slightly small… comparable to Nike" (Outsole). size.ly: Under Armour 10 is about Nike 11.
- **Clarks.**
  - Desert Boot, Zappos customers: 379 true to size, 174 a half size larger, 120 a full size larger.
  - Bootspy: Clarks "run larger than shoe brands like Nike or Vans".
  - Wallabee: SoleFeed says it "often runs large".
- **Dr. Martens.**
  - Whole sizes only. Scheels: "If you are a half size, we recommend going down."
  - Stridewise: the 1460 should be "one whole size from your true size (1.5 sizes from your sneaker size)".
  - Foot-length charts disagree: LookSize prints men's US 7 at 26.3 cm, while a search summary quotes Bergfreunde at 25 cm. I saved Bergfreunde's page but did not parse it.
- **Birkenstock.**
  - Foot Locker UK: "tend to run slightly large, so we recommend sizing down".
  - Zappos, Arizona Birko-Flor: 804 true to size, 94 half larger, 82 full larger, 19 half smaller, 5 full smaller.
- **Timberland.** Stridewise: 6-inch Premium "a whole size down from your sneaker size"; the author is Brannock 11.75, sneaker 11.5, boot 11. SoleFeed: "size down at least half a size". RunRepeat: some boots run shorter or longer than the brand's chart.
- **UGG.** AlpinStore: Classics "go down half a size, or even a full size", Tasman and Neumel true to size. SoleFeed: Classic Mini half a size down. Zappos, Classic Short: 275 true, 64 half larger, 55 full larger, 42 half smaller, 21 full smaller.
- **Crocs.**
  - Zappos, Classic Clog: 2,887 true, 335 half larger, 215 full larger, 224 half smaller, 169 full smaller.
  - Slickdeals, as printed: "some say they run small, while others say they run large." One comment there says to size up, another says the writer usually sizes down.
- **HOKA.**
  - Consistent with New Balance and adidas (RunRepeat); "true to size" (Outsole, Fit2Run).
  - Its women's sizes are reported to sit one US size above men's (RunRepeat) and 1.5 (Outsole), so the two disagree.
- **On.** RunRepeat: "brand itself recommends getting a half size bigger in certain silhouettes". Tom's Guide went half a size up in Cloudmonster. Zappos, Cloudmonster: 160 true, 17 half larger, 5 full larger, 15 half smaller, 17 full smaller.
- **Brooks.** Repeats the brand's own "half-a-size to a full size larger than your casual dress shoe" (RunRepeat, Scheels). Footwear Magazine adds that this "does not mean you must automatically go up another size from a Hoka".
- **Salomon.**
  - RunRepeat: "Based on our wear tests and lab measurements… run true to size". It also says there is "a 5-mm difference between a foot length and a Mondopoint (CM) size".
  - Outsole: "slightly tighter than HOKA".
  - SportsShoes's women's table prints UK 3.5 at 31.5 heel-to-toe, which looks like a typo on their page.

## Summary

1. **Answer:** no third-party source measured feet against brand sizes. RunRepeat, size.ly, AlpinStore and Footwear Magazine compile from the brands' charts and say so. The retailers' and LookSize's charts are compiled without saying from where. The blogs, magazines and the one forum I reached only give opinions. Zappos's per-product customer fit counts are the only many-wearer data.
2. **Strongest finding:** the Zappos `sizeRatingCounts` data, for example 40,771 reviews on the Crocs Classic Clog (https://www.zappos.com/product/review/7153812) and the Desert Boot's lean toward "larger than marked" (https://www.zappos.com/product/review/105990). It is customers' reports per product, and the count of size answers is often far below the review total (Reebok Club C 85: 3,655 reviews, 167 size answers).
3. **Strongest finding:** the two compiled foot-length cross-walks disagree. size.ly puts Nike a full size off New Balance at the same foot (https://www.size.ly/shoe-size-recommender/nike-to-new-balance); RunRepeat puts men's Nike and New Balance equal (https://runrepeat.com/size-charts/nike). That is a lead to check against the brands' own charts.
4. **Strongest finding:** Converse Chucks half a size large is the most consistent claim across sources (RunRepeat's table, Outsole, OPUMO, Man of Many, Captain Creps; https://runrepeat.com/size-charts/converse).
5. **Third-party charts:** 5,244 cells from 10 sources in `charts.csv`. Where a column is labelled foot, the record says so; where it is only "CM" or "Length", it is flagged unlabelled.
6. **Could not reach:** Reddit (three ways), QVC, The Iconic and eBay, plus guides that do not exist at the URLs tried (GOAT, Stadium Goods, JD Sports, several brand pages).
7. **Tool calls:** about 117 of the 150 budget; I stopped gathering at about 113. Every claim was read by a script from a saved file; the fetcher was tried once and refused.
8. **What's in the files:** 134 claims and 43 Zappos product rows. The page log in `sources.md` covers 140 pages logged by the fetch script plus the 60 files `zappos.py` saved.
9. **Repository:** only `docs/RESEARCH.md` sections 1–3 were read; nothing was written there.
