## 2. The method in RTINGS' words

- Test size, how-we-test (https://www.rtings.com/running-shoes/learn/how-we-test, updated Oct 02, 2026; the page warns it "isn't fully up to date"): "These measurements are specific to our test size (a men's US size 9)." and "Shoe weight, shape, fit, and stack heights can vary between sizes or even between men's and women's versions".
- The exception, Test Bench 0.10 changelog (https://www.rtings.com/running-shoes/tests/changelogs/0-10, updated Apr 24, 2026): "For all new shoes that we test, these measurements will be taken on a men's US size 9. For some shoes we had already reviewed before adding this test bench, measurements were taken on men's US sizes 8 and 13." The sentence is about the 3D interior scans (widths and heights); it does not say which shoes, nor whether the footbed internal length was taken on the same pair. It calls the internal-shape figures "our existing footbed measurements".
- Internal length, the table's own tooltip on a review (https://www.rtings.com/running-shoes/reviews/nike/pegasus-41): "The footbed's length measured from the heel's back to the toe box's end." Good value: "A typical men's size 9 shoe has an internal length of about 270 mm."
- Heel width: "The width of the footbed at 12% of the internal length." Forefoot width: "Width of the footbed at 75% of the internal length."
- Instrument: how-we-test names the Artec Space Spider 3D scanner for shape and "a mold of the shoe's interior" for fit; it does not say the internal length is read from the scan or the mold, how many pairs, or any variance. One pair is kept for lab tests and another for running ("our tester always uses one dedicated pair of each shoe for lab testing").
- Size of a given shoe: the Pegasus 41 review says "We tested the Nike Pegasus 41 in men's US size 9, as noted on the label." (Pegasus 41 is on the 0.10 retest list, so at least this pre-0.10 shoe is a 9.) Three other reviews opened (Nike Streakfly, Crocs Classic Clog, Brooks Ghost Max 2) state no size.
- No internal-shape test article was found: /running-shoes/tests redirects to /research, /running-shoes/tests/design/internal-shape is a 404, and the changelog index has none titled for it.
